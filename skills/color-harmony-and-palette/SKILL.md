# Colour harmony and palette

## 60-30-10 <!-- core -->
60 % dominant ground (light brand tint, white, or deep brand colour); 30 % structural type and rules in ONE tone with strong contrast to the
ground; 10 % a single accent hue for the one thing that must pop (call to action, key date, pill, icons). Never pure black, never a
rainbow: all body lines share one structural colour.

## Accent on the right ground <!-- core -->
The brand accent is not always readable. A light accent (a mint or teal) works on a dark ground and fails on white; on a light ground use
the brand's tertiary or its primary for accents. Rule used by the agent: accent colour on grounds darker than about 25 % luminance, the
tertiary or primary on lighter grounds, and only if the contrast is at least 4.5:1.

## Roles for text
Headline: structural tone or the accent (large text needs 3:1). Subtitle and organiser: the structural tone, slightly softened. Dates and
lineup: structural tone. Call to action: the accent. Consecutive lines never get different hues.

## Art and palette
Generated art is described in the brand's own hues so the ground and the type belong together; the art's calm areas are where text goes.
Checkable: at most one accent hue; text contrast at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for display type); no more than three hues in total besides neutrals.
