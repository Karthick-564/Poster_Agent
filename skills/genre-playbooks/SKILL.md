# Genre playbooks

## Workshop or training <!-- genres: workshop -->
Composition: header row + left column, or tab and rows. Type: editorial or bold. Structure: a list of days or topics as an IconRow or
Columns, a pill for certificates or fees, a footer band with registration. Imagery: a calm generated background in the brand's hues, or
none. Must show: dates, time, venue, how to register. Avoid: a plain white page of centred lines.

## Recruitment or club drive <!-- genres: recruitment -->
Composition: tab and rows, or split. Type: bold or condensed. Structure: a hook line in the top tab, a big title, domains as an IconRow,
a QR block with a call to action when a URL exists, a deadline in the footer band. Imagery: team photo with PhotoFade if supplied.

## Admission or programme <!-- genres: admission -->
Composition: split or title low over a photo. Type: bold. Structure: programme name large inside a pill or band, a short list of
highlights, contact and URL in the footer band, QR. Imagery: a supplied campus or student photo (duotone in brand colours if mixed).

## Conference or symposium <!-- genres: conference -->
Composition: split or header row. Type: classic or editorial. Structure: title block, a speaker grid or a list of tracks, a details band.
Photos of speakers only if supplied. Avoid: more than six groups of information.

## Talk, seminar or lecture <!-- genres: talk -->
Composition: header row or title low. Type: editorial or elegant. Structure: title, speaker name and designation (verbatim), details in a
quiet band. Imagery: soft generated background; calm and spacious.

## Cultural festival or concert <!-- genres: cultural -->
Composition: big word or title low. Type: elegant or bold. Structure: few boxes; type sits on atmospheric art with a soft scrim; one warm
accent. Imagery: a generated background and optionally a single object hero cutout (never people).

## Sports <!-- genres: sports -->
Composition: tab and rows or title low. Type: condensed or bold. Structure: events as an IconRow, dates and venue in a band. Imagery: a
generated track or ground from above in warm light, abstract, no people.

## Commemoration, reunion, convocation, achievement <!-- genres: commemoration -->
Composition: big word (a number or year) or centred stack for the most formal ones. Type: elegant. Structure: restrained; navy and one
accent; a double border. Imagery: a pale luminous textured ground.

## Placement or career event <!-- genres: placement -->
Composition: tab and rows. Type: classic or bold. Structure: eligibility and process as an icon row or columns, deadline pill, contact
band. Avoid: a plain page; use a header band and an accent.

## Launch or announcement <!-- genres: launch -->
Composition: big word or title low. Type: bold or editorial. Structure: one dominant line, one supporting line, date and venue band.
