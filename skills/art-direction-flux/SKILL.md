# Art direction for generated art

## Rules <!-- core -->
- Describe what IS there, in positive prose; do not list things to avoid (the image model treats a named thing as a request for it).
- The art contains no words, letters, numbers, signs, screens or people: describe light, material, texture and shape only. Use words like
  mist, soft light, gradient field, ripples, fabric folds, paper layers, light rays.
- Name a design movement or material instead of empty adjectives ("modern, sleek, professional" produce generic gradients): Swiss grid,
  Bauhaus geometry, risograph grain, paper-cut layers, soft studio light.
- Calm zones: state where the art is quiet and low in detail so type can sit there (upper third, lower third). The text blocks decide it.
- Palette: use the brand's own hues for the main colours of the art, and keep it light or dark to match the tone the type needs.
- Light: one direction and temperature for the whole image; a hero cutout uses the same light as the background.
- A hero cutout is one isolated object with a clean silhouette, never a person or a logo.

## Photo-first ladder <!-- core -->
Supplied photos and brand fields come first; generated art is the fallback. A supplied photo may be cropped, faded into the ground or
given a brand duotone; it is never regenerated or retouched.
